# Status badge

A one- or two-word state with a coloured dot, used in tables, tile corners and lists.

- Five states, mapped to tokens: good `status-good`, warning `status-warn`, bad `status-bad`, info `status-info`, neutral `status-neutral`.
- The dot carries the colour; the background is the matching `-soft` tint; **the text stays `text`**, so every badge passes contrast in both themes (red text on dark would only be 3.7:1).
- The word must carry the meaning on its own ("Down", not just a red dot) — about 1 in 12 men can't tell red from green.
- Status colours are reserved: never use green, orange or red for a category or a brand accent on the same view.
